How was Zobo (Hibiscus) used for heart health in the 1950s?

In my grandmother's time, we prepared Zobo as a drink and as medicine. For the heart, we would steep the dried calyces overnight with ginger and a touch of honey. This was taken warm in the morning, especially for those with high blood pressure...

Kano State, Hausa Tradition · Dala District
